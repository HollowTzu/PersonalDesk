COT vs. ETF-Flow Confluence

Reference guide

COT Managed Money is fast, leveraged futures positioning. ETF flow (a price/volume proxy here) reflects slower, more strategic demand. Comparing them tells you which type of buyer is actually driving a move.

The Four Combinations

COTETF FlowRead
↑↑Strongest confluence — broad-based demand.
↑=/↓Fragile rally — speculative only, reverses hard.
↓↑Quiet accumulation — stealth-strength signal.
↓↓Broad distribution — weakest environment.

Real Yields & Breakevens

Real yield = nominal yield − breakeven. Gold's opportunity cost is set by the real yield, not the nominal one.

NominalBreakevenRead
↑=Real yield rising (tightening) — bearish gold.
↑↑↑Real yield may still fall — neutral-to-bullish, possible debasement hedge.
↓=Real yield falling (growth scare) — bullish.

Gold stayed elevated through much of 2026 even with real yields relatively high — a partial decoupling from the textbook relationship, usually signaling something structural (central bank reserve buying) overriding the mechanics.
